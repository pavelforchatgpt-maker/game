# LOW-PLAYER ACTIVATION CADENCE DIAGNOSTIC

Date: 2026-11-10.

Status: diagnostic only. Canonical rules unchanged.

## Setup

Ordinary randomness, global planner, ST-NEUTRAL, approved hero profiles cycled across combinations.

20-day deadline. No movement, energy, map or capital-DC changes.

A diagnostic extra full activation was granted to the single 2p/3p group every Nth day.

## Results

| Players | Extra activation cadence | Wins / N | Win rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | every 5 days | 0/512 | 0.0% |
| 2 | every 4 days | 1/512 | 0.2% |
| 2 | every 3 days | 0/512 | 0.0% |
| 2 | every 2 days | 4/512 | 0.8% |
| 2 | every day | 128/1024 | 12.5% |
| 3 | every 5 days | 0/512 | 0.0% |
| 3 | every 4 days | 1/512 | 0.2% |
| 3 | every 3 days | 2/512 | 0.4% |
| 3 | every 2 days | 7/512 | 1.4% |
| 3 | every day | 244/1024 | 23.8% |

Daily second activation:
- 2p mean winning day 18.43, median 19;
- 3p mean winning day 18.57, median 19.

## Interpretation

Sparse bonus activations do not solve the action-economy bottleneck. The response is strongly nonlinear: even every second day is far too weak, while a second activation every day produces a material increase.

A clean interpretation for further testing is two team activations per world day:
- 2p/3p: the single group may receive a second activation;
- 4p split: the two pairs naturally supply two group activations.

This is not yet an approved rule. Daily second activation alone still does not produce a robust win corridor across hero compositions, so the next diagnostic tests capital-check scaling on top of the equalized action economy.
