# CANDIDATE B — LARGE VALIDATION

Date: 2026-11-11.

Status: diagnostic only. Canonical rules unchanged.

## Candidate B

- one shared bonus team activation per world day;
- 2 players: capital DC -2;
- 3 players: capital DC -2;
- 4 players in a pair: capital DC -1;
- 4 players together: canonical capital DC;
- final DC, energy 4/6/8, map, movement and Day 20 unchanged.

The shared bonus activation belongs to the team, not to every group. With 4p split, the team has two normal group activations plus one shared bonus activation that one group receives.

## Workload / duration proxy

Candidate B adds activations, but does not proportionally add content-heavy POI scenes.

Mean movement rolls to Day 20:
- 2p: 72.0
- 3p: 108.7
- 4p split: 107.7
- 4p dynamic: 108.2

Mean POI:
- 2p: 8.03
- 3p: 12.08
- 4p split: 18.67
- 4p dynamic: 19.26

Therefore the additional action economy is spent mainly on travel and capital throughput, not on doubling the amount of event-card reading/resolution. Actual tabletop minutes cannot be inferred reliably until a physical timed playtest, but the workload does not support a naive “twice as many activations = twice as long” assumption.

3p:
- control: 0.0%
- travel-only bonus: 0.0%

- capital-only opportunity: 0.0%
- full bonus activation: 23.4% with canonical capital DC

Interpretation: the large improvement is not generated by one isolated component. It requires both general action throughput and a manageable capital burden. This is why Candidate B combines the shared bonus activation with player-count-sensitive capital DC.

Travel-only is not a clean standalone balance estimate because the current route planner overuses the unrestricted travel opportunity and can move away from useful capital work; treat its 0% as “not a viable policy in this implementation,” not as proof that travel has zero value.
